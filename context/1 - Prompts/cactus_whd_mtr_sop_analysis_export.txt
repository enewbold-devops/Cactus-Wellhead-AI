SECTION 1 — CACTUS WELLHEAD BUSINESS CONTEXT
======================================================================

Cactus Wellhead (Cactus, Inc. / NYSE: WHD) is an equipment and field-services
business focused on surface pressure control across the full well lifecycle:
drilling, completion/fracking, and production. The company's second business leg
covers spoolable pipe technologies (FlexSteel).

Core Product and Service Buckets:
  - Wellhead systems (notably the SafeDrill® line)
  - Frac equipment (frac stacks, zipper manifolds)
  - Production trees and related surface equipment
  - Field service / technicians supporting installs, swaps, maintenance, and frac ops

Strategic Context (as of early 2026):
  Cactus acquired 65% of Baker Hughes' surface pressure control business, effective
  January 1, 2026, forming "Cactus International." This significantly expands the
  company's international footprint, particularly in the Middle East within the
  NOC/IOC aftermarket segment.

How the Business Wins:
  - Cycle time and safety in pressure control execution
  - Standardization and interchangeability of components across basins
  - Service density proximity to key shale basins
  - International mix to smooth regional activity cycles

Engineering Context:
  Cactus products are governed by demanding material and quality requirements
  including:
    - API 6A / ISO 10423 (PSL, temperature class, material class)
    - NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 (sour service compliance)
    - Customer-specific engineering performance specs (EPS)
    - Internal Cactus Master Specifications (CMS)

  The "big five" controlled characteristics on all pressure-bearing material are:
    1) Chemical composition (chemistry gating)
    2) Mechanical properties (yield, tensile, elongation, reduction in area)
    3) Charpy impact (CVN) — method, temperature, orientation, acceptance
    4) Heat treatment parameters — temperature, soak, quench, temper, re-HT limits
    5) Hardness — scale, location, frequency, method, NACE caps where applicable

SECTION 2 — SUPPLIER ENGAGEMENT AND MTR INTAKE WORKFLOW
======================================================================

Cactus receives MTR packets from two classes of counterparties:
  A) Customers submitting MTRs for incoming material (customer-supplied stock)
  B) Suppliers shipping material with an MTR/CoC packet

The intake workflow is unified across both source types, then risk-branched.

---
2.1 Intake Baseline (All Sources)
---

Step 1 — Packet Receipt and Case Creation:
  - Create an "MTR Validation Case" record: counterparty, PO/line, part, heat/lot,
    spec/grade, material form.
  - Ingest all attachments: PDF (text-based, scanned/OCR, hybrid, portfolio),
    DOCX/XLSX, images (JPG/PNG/TIFF/HEIC), ZIP bundles, MSG/EML email packages.
  - Malware scan, SHA-256 hash, and archive original files before any processing.
  - Normalize: OCR for scanned PDFs, rotation/deskew for images, table extraction.

Step 2 — Gate 0: Packet Completeness Check:
  Minimum required data before technical review proceeds:
    - Heat/cast number
    - PO number and line item
    - Part or material identifier
    - Governing spec/grade + revision
    - Chemistry table (per applicable CMS)
    - Mechanical properties (tensile/yield/elongation/RA)
    - Charpy results (when required by PO/EPS)
    - Heat treatment actuals (when required)
    - Hardness results (method, location, values)
    - NDT certificate reference (when applicable)
    - Test lab or provider identification
  If incomplete: return as "incomplete packet" with specific punch list; do not
  begin technical validation.

Step 3 — Gate 1: Identity and Traceability:
  - Confirm heat number on MTR matches: PO, receiving record, physical tag.
  - Confirm product form and dimensions match PO + receiving inspection.
  - Confirm spec/grade matches PO line and any customer flowdown.
  Common rejection reasons at this gate:
    - Heat number mismatch or cross-reference without certification
    - Wrong grade or trim level (e.g., AISI 4130 submitted when 4140 required)
    - Multiple heats aggregated without clear segregation and individual certs

---
2.2 Queue-Based Ownership Model
---

  QUEUE                    RESPONSIBLE PARTY          FUNCTION
  -----------------        -----------------------    --------------------------
  Intake / Doc Control     Receiving Admin / QMS      Packet completeness (Gate 0)
  Receiving QC             Inspector / QC Tech        Identity + traceability (Gate 1)
  QE / Metallurgy          Quality Engineer           Technical validation (Gate 2/3)
  Quality Manager          QE or QM                  Deviation disposition
  Records Control          Doc Control                Final lock + archive (Gate 4)

---
2.3 MTR File Format Handling (Supplier/Customer Docs)
---

  File type complexity encountered in practice:

  A. PDFs:
     - Text-based PDFs (from ERP/QMS): best case — fully searchable
     - Scanned PDFs: image-only, require OCR extraction
     - Hybrid PDFs: OCR text layer may have alignment or encoding artifacts
     - PDF portfolios / multi-doc binders: multiple certs in one container

  B. Office Documents:
     - DOCX: certificate templates with typed values + signature blocks
     - XLSX: chemistry + tensile + impact tables; high value if true tables present
     - Issues: merged cells, hidden rows, macros, "printed-to-PDF" derivatives

  C. Images:
     - JPG/PNG/TIFF/HEIC from phone cameras, scanners, bundle tags
     - Issues: perspective distortion, glare, low DPI, missing corners

  D. Package Formats:
     - ZIP bundles of full MTR packets
     - MSG/EML email attachments (intact or forwarded chains)

  All formats must route through a normalize-then-validate pipeline before any
  automated field extraction or CMS-gated validation is applied.

---
2.4 Disposition Outcomes
---

  - ACCEPTED: all Gates pass, records locked to validated case
  - ACCEPTED WITH CONDITIONS: minor open items with defined resolution path
  - REJECTED: material fails spec limits or traceability cannot be established
  - DEVIATION REQUIRED: out-of-spec but potentially acceptable via SDR/waiver process

  No silent substitutions: any change after PO placement requires formal deviation
  (SDR/EPS waiver) with Quality signature.

---
2.5 Supplier Performance Loop
---

  Track continuously per supplier and per supplier site:
    - First-pass acceptance rate (by spec / by material family)
    - Top failure causes (Charpy gap, heat number mismatch, hardness method wrong, etc.)
    - Average queue turnaround time by gate
    - Repeat non-conformances by supplier/site/document template
    - SDR/waiver frequency and cycle time

  Feed results back to supplier qualification and risk scoring.

SECTION 3 — SOP DEEP DIVE: CMS SPECS + APPROVED PROVIDER CORRELATION
======================================================================

The CMS validation framework defines which Cactus Master Specifications govern
a given material, and which approved process providers are authorized to generate
or attest to the required test data.

The governing sequence for every MTR validation case is:

  STEP 1 — Resolve applicable CMS set from PO + material/part mapping:
    Example A: 4140 forgings → CMS-002 + CMS-107 (+ CMS-004 if Charpy required)
    Example B: Alloy 718 → CMS-002 + CMS-305 (+ CMS-004 for Charpy)
    Example C: 17-4PH NACE → CMS-002 + CMS-303

  STEP 2 — For each required test line item on the MTR, extract:
    - Test parameter name and value
    - Test method and revision
    - Provider (lab/HT/NDT supplier name + site)
    - Equipment calibration reference (when applicable)

  STEP 3 — Match provider to approved provider list:
    - Match on: supplier_name + site_city/address + process_code + approval_status
    - Status must be ACTIVE
    - Site-level match required — company name alone is NOT sufficient
    - If notes contain "SINGLE SOURCE — risk review required":
        route to risk review queue before acceptance
    - If notes contain "ADDRESS NEEDS MANUAL REVIEW":
        route to Quality hold for manual verification before acceptance

  STEP 4 — Gate 2/3: Technical validation against CMS limits for each resolved spec.
    Each CTQ (Critical to Quality) is checked element by element:
      Chemistry: element vs. min/max table in CMS
      Mechanicals: YS, UTS, elongation, RA vs. grade limits
      Charpy: temperature, specimen, orientation, average/individual vs. CMS-004 rules
      Heat treat: austenitizing temp, quench media, temper temp, soak time vs. HTP
      Hardness: scale (Rockwell/Brinell/Vickers), location, method vs. CMS rule
      NDE: applicable method, acceptance class, applicable CNT-* code authorized

  STEP 5 — Gate 4: Record lock.
    Link accepted case to:
      - CMS revision (exact) + cutoff/applicability date
      - Approved provider ID(s) matched
      - Heat/lot + PO line
      - Disposition outcome + dispositioner identity + date

SECTION 4 — CMS-TO-APPROVED-PROCESS CROSSWALK (NARRATIVE + TABLE)
======================================================================

---
CMS-002: Base Requirements (Forgings and Wrought Products)
---
  Scope: Foundational material requirements applied to all forgings, tubulars,
  bars, and mill shapes procured for pressure-bearing components. Always active.

  Mandatory process codes:
    LAB-001 — accredited lab for chemistry, tensile, Charpy, and hardness
    CHT-GEN — general heat treat supplier capability

  Conditional process codes (per PO or EPS):
    CNT-002, CNT-003, CNT-004, CNT-005, CNT-007 — NDT method as flowed

  Required MTR evidence:
    Chemistry, tensile/mechanicals, Charpy (if required), hardness,
    heat treat actuals, heat/lot-to-PO traceability.

  Key SOP note:
    CMS-002 takes precedence over any conflicting requirement in a material-specific
    CMS. CMS-002-1 applies specifically to API 6A PSL-3 75K yield bodies over 1000 lbs
    during heat treating.

---
CMS-004: Charpy / CVN Impact Framework
---
  Scope: Charpy test parameters, sub-size correlation rules, and acceptance logic.
  Applied in conjunction with CMS-002 and the applicable material-specific CMS
  whenever Charpy is called out by PO or EPS.

  Mandatory process codes:
    LAB-001 — must be explicitly scoped for CVN impact testing

  Key SOP note:
    Do not assume LAB-001 approval for tensile testing automatically covers Charpy.
    Verify impact testing capability is explicitly listed in the approved scope for
    the specific lab site.

---
CMS-107: 4140 Low-Alloy Steel
---
  Scope: Material-specific requirements for AISI 4140 class; typically the most
  common material family in pressure-control forgings. Tight chemistry and HT
  window controls.

  Mandatory process codes:
    LAB-001, CHT-GEN

  Conditional process codes:
    CNT-005 (UT), additional CNT-* per flowdown

  Required MTR evidence:
    Chemistry vs. CMS-107 element tables, Q&T route actuals (austenitize/quench/
    temper temps, soak, quench media), tensile + impact + hardness results,
    HT furnace record reference.

  Key SOP note:
    Hardness caps apply. For sour-service (NACE/ISO 15156) flowdowns, validate
    maximum hardness limits against NACE tables in addition to CMS-107 limits.

---
CMS-118: 4340 Low-Alloy Steel
---
  Scope: Material-specific requirements for AISI 4340 class; higher hardenability
  than 4140. More sensitive to section size and quench media control.

  Mandatory process codes:
    LAB-001, CHT-GEN

  Conditional process codes:
    CNT-005, additional CNT-* per PO/EPS

  Required MTR evidence:
    Chemistry, HT window actuals, tensile/impact/hardness, heat/lot traceability.

  Key SOP note:
    Confirm HT supplier approval is at the site level — heavy section 4340 quench
    capability is site-specific. Company-level approval alone is insufficient.

---
CMS-303: 17-4PH Precipitation Hardening Stainless — NACE Route
---
  Scope: Requirements for 17-4PH (UNS S17400) including corrosion-resistant and
  NACE-compliant aging condition routes.

  Mandatory process codes:
    LAB-001, CHT-GEN

  Conditional process codes:
    CNT-002, CNT-003, CNT-004, CNT-005, CNT-007 per PO/EPS

  Required MTR evidence:
    Full chemistry including Cu and Nb/Cb, ferrite / microstructure evaluation
    (where required), aging condition actuals (H900/H1025/H1150-M etc.),
    hardness (method + limits), traceability.

  Key SOP note:
    Validate the aging condition on the MTR matches the PO/EPS requirement exactly.
    For NACE route: verify hardness cap compliance and modified chemistry limits.
    Ferrite and delta ferrite controls may be required — confirm LAB-001 microscopy
    and metallography scope at the site level.

---
CMS-305: Alloy 718 / API 6ACRA / NACE Route
---
  Scope: Requirements for UNS N07718 (Alloy 718) following API 6ACRA requirements
  for corrosion-resistant alloy pressure-control equipment. Highest-scrutiny spec
  in the CMS suite.

  Mandatory process codes:
    LAB-001, CHT-GEN

  Conditional process codes:
    CNT-005, additional CNT-* as specified

  Required MTR evidence:
    Full chemistry (Nb, Mo, Ti, Al trace elements included), Charpy impact results,
    hardness (method, location, limits), heat treat route actuals (solution anneal
    + double age temperatures and soak times), macroetch evaluation, microstructure
    phase examination (deleterious phase check), API 6ACRA traceability artifacts.

  Key SOP note:
    Confirm LAB-001 scope explicitly covers: ASTM A604 (macroetch), ASTM E1181
    (grain size), metallography, and phase evaluation. Standard mechanical lab
    scope does not cover these.
    API 6ACRA traceability chain must be unbroken from melt source through
    finished forging form.
    Verify CHT-GEN and CNT-005 are not single-source for this material route.

---
COATING_PLATING: Surface Treatment Requirements
---
  Scope: Coating and plating process requirements when specified by PO or EPS.
  Only activated for parts with an explicit coating or plating requirement.

  Mandatory process codes (as applicable per PO):
    CCS-608, CCS-609, CCS-610, CCS-612
    CST-800, CST-801, CST-811

  Required MTR/cert evidence:
    Coating/plating process certs, thickness measurements, process parameters
    (bath chemistry, cure, bake relief), lot traceability to part/PO line.

  Key SOP note:
    Validate at site level — company name alone insufficient.
    CCS-612 and CST-811 are flagged as single-source — require risk review before
    acceptance on any order.
    If the primary supplier outsources coating/plating to a sub-tier, that sub-tier
    must also be on the Cactus approved provider list under the applicable
    process code.

SECTION 5 — KEY RISKS IDENTIFIED
======================================================================

  1. INCOMPLETE ADDRESS RECORDS IN APPROVED PROVIDER LIST
     Multiple provider entries are flagged "ADDRESS NEEDS MANUAL REVIEW."
     These cannot be validated at the site level until address data is confirmed.
     Recommendation: quarantine these records; do not auto-approve provider matches
     against entries without verified site address until manual review is complete.

  2. SINGLE-SOURCE PROCESS RISKS
     The following process codes have single-source providers in the approved list:
       - CCS-612 (coating)
       - CHT-100 (heat treat — specific capability)
       - CST-811 (surface treatment)
       - CNT-007 (NDT method)
     Any order requiring these services must trigger a supply chain risk review.
     A second-source qualification plan should be initiated for each.

  3. COMPANY-LEVEL VS. SITE-LEVEL APPROVAL GAPS
     Parent-company HT delegations exist in the approved provider list.
     The SOP must enforce that approval is validated at the individual operating
     site (street address + city), and not assumed from a corporate parent entry.
     This is especially critical for CHT-GEN (heat treat) and LAB-001 (testing lab).

  4. LAB SCOPE COVERAGE GAPS (CMS-303 and CMS-305)
     LAB-001 approval in the list may be granted for standard mechanical testing
     (tensile/Charpy/hardness) without explicitly covering:
       - Macroetch (ASTM A604)
       - Grain size (ASTM E1181)
       - Metallographic phase evaluation (deleterious phase detection)
     These are required by CMS-305 and conditionally by CMS-303. Confirm scope
     certificate lists these capabilities before routing Alloy 718 or 17-4PH
     NACE MTRs to the lab for validation.

  5. MISSING PROVIDER MATCH = NOT AUTO-REJECT
     If a provider cannot be matched to the approved list, the correct action is
     to route to "Needs Review" (Quality hold), not an automatic rejection.
     The provider may be legitimately approved under a different record, or may
     require a one-time qualification for the specific scope.

SECTION 6 — SOP ENFORCEMENT RULES (APPENDIX CONTENT)
======================================================================

  RULE 1: Provider match is required at the site level.
    Required match fields: supplier_name + site address/city + process_code +
    approval_status = ACTIVE.
    Company name + process code alone is NOT sufficient.

  RULE 2: "ADDRESS NEEDS MANUAL REVIEW" entries trigger Quality hold.
    No MTR can be auto-accepted against a provider record with this flag active.
    Route to Quality Engineer for manual address verification before disposition.

  RULE 3: "SINGLE SOURCE — risk review required" entries trigger risk queue.
    Add risk review step before final acceptance.
    Document risk review outcome in MTR Validation Case record.

  RULE 4: Test method present but provider code missing or mismatched.
    Disposition = "Conditional / Needs Review."
    Quality Engineer to resolve before final disposition.

  RULE 5: All accepted cases must be locked to:
    - CMS spec + revision + applicability date
    - Approved provider record ID(s)
    - Heat/lot number + PO line item
    - Disposition outcome + dispositioner + disposition date

  RULE 6: No silent changes.
    Any change to material, source, process, or test result after PO placement
    requires a formal SDR (Supplier Deviation Request) or EPS waiver with
    documented Quality disposition.

  RULE 7: CMS-002 always applies.
    For any forging or wrought material purchase, CMS-002 is the floor.
    CMS-004 is applied whenever Charpy is required. Material-specific CMS
    (107/118/303/305) layers on top. Conflicts resolve to CMS-002.

  RULE 8: Approved sub-tier traceability for outsourced processes.
    If a primary supplier outsources any process (heat treat, coating, NDT,
    lab testing) to a sub-tier, that sub-tier must appear in the Cactus
    approved provider list under the applicable process code and site.
