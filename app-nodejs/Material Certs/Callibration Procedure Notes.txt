Purpose

Establishes the specific calibration recall frequency for testing, measuring, monitoring, and detection
equipment (TMMDE) at Cactus Wellhead. Only tools that are within current calibration, shall be used
to perform all planned monitoring and measurement activities for Dimensional Inspection for API 6A
Products.
NOTE: If these recall frequencies differ from the frequencies referenced in specific Cactus Wellhead
calibration procedures, the Cactus Wellhead specific procedure will be followed.
References
 Cactus Management System Procedure MSP 5.8, Control of Testing, Measuring, Monitoring, and
Detection Equipment
 Cactus Wellhead CCP-XXX series Calibration Procedures
NOTE: The latest effective editions of the above standards and specification are included by reference
in this work instruction.

Establishing Calibration Cycles

 Calibration frequencies shall be established based on the manufactures established
frequencies, industrial standard frequencies, or established national or international standard
frequencies.
 The calibration frequency for pressure measurement instruments, that are used for final
acceptance of product, shall be set at 3 months until a recorded history is established.
 Calibration frequencies for pressure measurement instruments, can be increased by 3-month
intervals based on a recorded history of stability and shall be shortened based on recorded calibration history. Extension of intervals shall be limited to 3-month increases, with a
maximum calibration interval to not exceed 1 year.
 Calibration intervals and when they begin shall be established in accordance with MSP 5.8.
 A recorded history of stability for pressure measurement instruments shall be defined as 1
cycle of calibration with less than a .5% variant of the Master to Test pressure readings as
recorded on the initial calibration certificate.
 Welding Control calibration frequencies shall be a maximum of 6 months until recorded
calibration history can be established. Extension of frequencies shall be limited to 6-month
increases, with a maximum calibration interval to not exceed 1 year.
